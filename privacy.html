DropPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

DropPilot is a locally run tool for product sourcing, listing workflow support, and order operations. This policy describes how the tool handles data when connected to services you authorize.

Information handled

DropPilot may handle store authorization credentials, product details, inventory availability, pricing, listing information, and order or shipment information needed for its configured workflow.

How information is used

Information is used only to operate the features you configure, such as sourcing products, preparing listings, and tracking orders. DropPilot does not sell personal information.

Where information is stored

DropPilot stores its local configuration and workflow data on the computer operated by the account holder. Credentials should be kept in local secret files and must not be shared or committed to public repositories.

Third-party services

When you connect eBay, CJ, Shopify, WooCommerce, or another service, that service's own terms and privacy policy also apply.

Your choices

You can disconnect a service by removing its local authorization credentials and revoking access through that service's account settings.

Contact

For questions about this tool's data handling, contact the operator of the DropPilot application through the connected seller account.
